# Engine: Trust-First Hook Formulas

## The 3-Second Rule (Trust Over Trickery)
The first 3 seconds must communicate:
1. **Calm Confidence**: Direct eye contact, relaxed shoulders, conversational start.
2. **Immediate Curiosity or Recognition**: Addressing an unspoken anxiety or real dilemma of an engaged couple or business owner.
3. **Zero Clickbait**: The hook must be an honest promise that the next 50 seconds directly delivers.

---

## 5 Core Hook Archetypes

### 1. The Behind-the-Lens Observation (Craft Truth)
- *"There is one moment in every wedding ceremony that most videographers miss because they are looking at the bride..."*
- *"If you are a business owner getting in front of a camera this month, do not make this lighting mistake."*
- *"Here is what 500 hours of video editing taught me about human confidence."*

### 2. The Counter-Intuitive Reframe
- *"The best wedding film you will ever watch is usually shot with the least amount of camera movement."*
- *"Why having an expensive 8K camera won't save a boring brand story."*
- *"You don't need charisma to look natural on camera — you just need to stop doing this one thing with your hands."*

### 3. "How I Wait" (Observational Hook)
- *"Watch how long I wait before I press record during a father-daughter dance."*
- *"Before we roll cameras on a $50k commercial, this is the 10-minute ritual I run with the founder."*
- *"The real art of wedding videography isn't directing people — it's waiting for them to forget you're in the room."*

### 4. The Client Defense (Protecting the Buyer)
- *"3 things a wedding videographer should tell you before you sign their contract, but rarely do."*
- *"Why big companies waste $20k on corporate videos that get 40 views (and how to fix it)."*
- *"The real reason you felt awkward in your last photoshoot — and it wasn't your fault."*

### 5. The Filmmaker Lesson / Micro-Story
- *"Last Saturday at 11:30 PM, the venue turned off the lights right before the couple's sparkler exit. Here’s what we did."*
- *"A CEO told me: 'I hate how my voice sounds on video'. Here is the 2-minute fix we used."*
