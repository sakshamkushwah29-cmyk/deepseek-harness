# Swarm Content Forge

Coordinates specialized roles through durable handoffs to produce consistent, high-trust Instagram content.

## Agent Swarm Roles
1. **Hunter Agent**: Identifies emerging questions, seasonal concerns, and real dilemmas from wedding planning communities and executive circles.
2. **Hook Crafter Agent**: Takes the angle and derives 5 distinct trust-building hooks (Behind-the-lens, Counter-intuitive, Observational, Client defense, Micro-story).
3. **Scriptwriter Agent**: Writes full conversational scripts featuring "One Long Hold" pacing, natural speaking cues, and unhurried dialogue.
4. **Director Agent**: Designs the visual shot-list, camera angles, lighting notes, and the signature `[How I Wait]` pause.
5. **Gatekeeper Agent**: Quality assurance step. Rejects cheap/fake promises, verifies that no hard-selling exists, and ensures tone is calm and educational.
