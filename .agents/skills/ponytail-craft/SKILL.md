# Ponytail Prompt Craft

> *"He says nothing. He writes one line. It works."*

## Guiding Principles
1. **Zero Fluff**: Eliminate preambles, apologies, conversational filler, and rhetorical bloat from prompt and model outputs.
2. **Deterministic Schemas**: Ensure scripts, shot-lists, and hooks follow rigorous machine-readable and creator-executable formats.
3. **High Leverage**: One precise constraint (e.g. `[Hold frame 6s silently without cutting]`) achieves 10x more cinematic quality than 5 paragraphs of vague adjectives.
