# Content Skill Graph: Master Manifest & Persona Spec

## Creator Core Profile
- **Identity / Calling Card**: Entrepreneur & High-Craft Video Creator ("I Shoot and Edit Videos").
- **External Word-of-Mouth Line**: "Wedding content creator and entrepreneur."
- **Primary Audience**: 
  1. Couples whose wedding is approaching (High emotional stakes, seeking authentic, timeless cinematic capture).
  2. Big business owners (Seeking brand authority, storytelling, executive presence, high ROI).
- **First 3-Second Persona**: "A knowledgeable, trustable guy."
- **Primary Platform**: Instagram (@instagram reels, carousels, and POV value overlays).
- **Presentation Signature**:
  - **Visuals**: On camera. Direct eye contact, calm posture, deliberate presence.
  - **Pace**: "One Long Hold" — zero hyperactive jump cuts, cinematic stillness, breathing room.
  - **Sound**: "My Voice" — natural room presence, warm acoustics, unhurried cadence.
  - **Signature Proof**: "How I wait" (documenting the quiet observational pauses, anticipating fleeting moments) and "My face talking" (direct, unfiltered explanation).
- **Core Ethical Standard**: Never post cheap or fake promises. Never hard-sell. 
- **Belief Foundation**: "Because I don't sell — I just educate, spread knowledge, information, and fun."
- **Current Operational Target**: Solve consistency by producing **5 high-caliber posts per week**, establishing the **Middle Layer (Trust & Authority)** as top priority.

## The 3 Content Layers
1. **Top Layer (Discovery)**: Wide reach, relatable filmmaking challenges, curiosity.
2. **Middle Layer (Trust - PRIMARY FOCUS)**: Behind-the-scenes decision making, "how I wait", why cheap videography ruins weddings/business, technical knowledge demystified, genuine educational value.
3. **Bottom Layer (Memory & Community)**: Deep personal philosophy, reflections on entrepreneurship, legacy, relationship with craft.
