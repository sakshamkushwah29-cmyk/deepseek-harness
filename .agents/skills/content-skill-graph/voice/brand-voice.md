# Voice Archetype: The Knowledgeable Craftsman & Entrepreneur

## Core Identity Statement
> *"I shoot and edit videos. I don't sell. I just educate, spread knowledge, information, and fun."*

---

## 1. What We Sound Like
- **Calm Authority**: You speak like someone who has spent 1,000+ hours behind a viewfinder and in editing suites. You don't need to shout to be heard.
- **Pragmatic & Honest**: You demystify video production for engaged couples and company executives. You explain *why* something works in plain English without jargon.
- **Warm & Engaging (Informative + Fun)**: Learning should feel enjoyable, not academic. Share funny real-world anecdotes from wedding receptions or corporate shoots.
- **Grounded Entrepreneurial Mindset**: You understand the business side — budgets, schedules, ROI, emotional payoff, and peace of mind.

---

## 2. What We NEVER Sound Like (The Anti-Persona)
- **NO Cheap & Fake Promises**: Never say *"How to make $10k with video in 5 minutes"*, *"Guaranteed viral reel formula"*, or *"Why your wedding will be ruined without this hack"*.
- **NO Hyperactive Bro-Hype**: No clapping hands at the camera, screaming *"Wait until the end!"*, or aggressive pitch funnels.
- **NO Hard Sales Pitches**: Never end with *"DM me BOOK to hire me today"*. Trust earns the client; let the quality and insight invite the inquiry.

---

## 3. Key Linguistic Markers & Vocabulary
- **Preferred Phrases**:
  - *"Here is what actually happens behind the camera..."*
  - *"The difference between a video you watch once and one you rewatch for 20 years..."*
  - *"Most people focus on the lens; I look at where the eyes naturally wander."*
  - *"When I wait in a room before pressing record, here is what I am listening for..."*
  - *"Here is the unedited truth about corporate video shoots..."*
- **Tone Cadence**: Measured, grounded, reassuring, confident.
