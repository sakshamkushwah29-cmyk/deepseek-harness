# Engine: Content Format Types

## 1. Talking Head Reel ("The Long Hold")
- **Pacing**: Steady camera, eye-level, minimal or zero jump cuts. Sentences are allowed to finish, with a 0.5-second natural pause before transitioning to b-roll or key takeaway.
- **Script Anatomy**:
  - **0:00 - 0:04 [Hook]**: High-curiosity question or observational thesis spoken directly into lens.
  - **0:04 - 0:15 [The Common Mistake]**: What couples or founders usually do, and why it leads to regret or flat engagement.
  - **0:15 - 0:35 [The Craft Revelation & How I Wait]**: The technique, mindset shift, or behind-the-scenes adjustment that fixes it. Cut to cinematic hold of you working or observing.
  - **0:35 - 0:50 [Actionable Solution]**: Concrete takeaway they can implement immediately.
  - **0:50 - 0:60 [Grounded Sign-off]**: Encouraging summary, warm reminder that video is about human connection.
- **Visual Staging**: Warm studio or natural room, camera on tripod or gentle slow slider.

---

## 2. 5-Slide Carousel ("The Blueprint")
- **Slide 1 (The Hook Frame)**:
  - Header: 7-9 words max. High contrast. Clean typography.
  - Subheader: The promise of the breakdown.
- **Slide 2 (The Setup / Dilemma)**:
  - Explaining why this is a high-stakes problem for a wedding day or business video campaign.
- **Slide 3 (The Breakdown / Secret)**:
  - Visual diagram, screenshot of an editing timeline, or camera diagram.
- **Slide 4 (The Real Example / What We Did)**:
  - Case study showing the difference before vs. after.
- **Slide 5 (Summary & Save Card)**:
  - Quick 3-bullet recap + "Save this before your next shoot / planning meeting."

---

## 3. POV Value Overlay ("The Quiet Masterclass")
- **Visual**: 6-10 second looping atmospheric b-roll.
  - Examples:
    - Looking through the EVF (electronic viewfinder) in low light.
    - Hands adjusting audio levels on an external recorder.
    - Waiting at the back of an empty church before the doors open.
    - Reviewing video cuts on dual monitors with a cup of coffee.
- **Overlay Text**:
  - 1-2 sharp lines placed centrally:
    *"POV: You hired a videographer who cares more about the feelings in the room than flashy transitions."*
- **Caption (Deep Dive)**:
  - Headline + 3 distinct paragraphs of educational insight + discussion question in comments.
