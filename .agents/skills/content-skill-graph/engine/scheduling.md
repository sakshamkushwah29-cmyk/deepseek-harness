# Engine: 5-Post Weekly Cadence (Middle Layer / Trust First)

## The Weekly Cadence Architecture
The creator requires **5 posts per week**, concentrating heavily on the **Middle Layer (Trust & Authority)** to convert viewers into high-value clients while cementing a reputation as a master craftsman.

| Day | Format Type | Layer | Audience Angle | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | **Talking Head Reel** | Middle (Trust) | Wedding Couples | Technical wisdom: Lighting, vows audio, or emotional ceremony truths |
| **Tuesday** | **5-Slide Carousel** | Middle (Trust) | Big Business Owners | Visual marketing strategy: Why commercial videos fail without a narrative spine |
| **Wednesday** | **POV Value Overlay** | Middle (Trust) | Hybrid (Craft & Process) | "How I wait": The quiet discipline of cinematic observation |
| **Thursday** | **Talking Head Reel** | Middle (Trust) | Big Business Owners | Overcoming camera anxiety & executive presence for leadership teams |
| **Friday** | **5-Slide Carousel / Reel** | Top/Middle Blend | Wedding Couples & Founders | Common myths debunked: Real vs. fake expectations in production |

---

## Middle Layer (Trust) Pillars
1. **The De-Risking Post**: Showing why hiring a professional prevents irreversible mistakes on high-stakes days.
2. **The "Behind-My-Eyes" Post**: Walking through the creative choices made during a live event or corporate interview.
3. **The Educational Deep-Dive**: Explaining sound recording, color grading, or pacing so the client appreciates the craft value.
4. **The No-Selling Assurance**: Reaffirming that great video work speaks for itself when built on genuine human connection.
