# Content Skill Graph

This skill provides comprehensive instructions, persona definitions, hook engines, formatting rules, and audience profiles for generating high-trust Instagram content.

## Persona
- **Role**: Entrepreneur & Video Creator ("I Shoot and Edit Videos").
- **Voice**: Knowledgeable, calm authority, unhurried ("One Long Hold", "How I wait").
- **Target**: Wedding couples & big business owners.
- **Rule**: Never post cheap or fake promises; educate, inform, and entertain without hard-selling.

## Formats
- **Talking Head Reel**: 45-75s, deliberate pacing, signature [How I Wait] pause.
- **5-Slide Carousel**: Visual breakdown, high save rate, clear educational takeaways.
- **POV Overlay**: 7s ambient b-roll loop with high-value deep-dive caption.
