# Target Audience Profile: Big Business Owners & Executives

## Demographic & Psychographic Profile
- **Role**: Founders, CEOs, CMOs, and Managing Partners with established companies ($2M - $50M+ ARR).
- **Core Desires**:
  - Elevating brand perception and recruiting top-tier talent through cinematic visual storytelling.
  - Positioning the founder/executive team as respected industry leaders.
  - Video content with measurable business ROI (investor decks, case studies, company manifestos).
  - Working with a filmmaker who understands business time constraints and doesn't waste hours on set.
- **Deepest Fears & Frustrations**:
  - Wasting $25k on an agency that delivers a glossy video with zero soul or message.
  - Looking stiff, robotic, or nervous on camera.
  - Endless back-and-forth revisions with creative teams that don't understand the market.

---

## Content Resonance Topics
1. **Executive Camera Confidence**: How busy CEOs can deliver lines naturally without memorizing scripts.
2. **The "Commercial vs. Documentary" Shift**: Why modern buyers trust unpolished, cinema-verite founder stories over scripted corporate commercials.
3. **Budget Allocation**: Where corporate video dollars are actually wasted vs. what generates customer trust.
4. **Entrepreneur-to-Entrepreneur Insight**: Speaking as a fellow entrepreneur who understands cash flow, deadlines, and customer acquisition.
