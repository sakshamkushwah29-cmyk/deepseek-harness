# Target Audience Profile: Couples Whose Wedding Is Coming

## Demographic & Psychographic Profile
- **Life Stage**: Planning an intimate, high-end, or destination wedding (typically 6-18 months out).
- **Core Desires**:
  - A wedding film that feels like a real motion picture, not a cheesy music video montage.
  - Genuine emotional preservation: Voices of grandparents, vows, trembling hands, unscripted tears.
  - A videographer who is discreet, calm, and doesn't turn their intimate ceremony into a noisy Hollywood set.
- **Deepest Fears & Anxieties**:
  - Feeling awkward and posed in front of cameras all day.
  - Video taking 9 months to deliver or looking outdated in 5 years.
  - Bad audio during the outdoor vows or toasts.
  - Missing the moments they were too overwhelmed to see.

---

## Content Resonance Topics
1. **Audio Integrity**: Why 50% of the film's emotion is microphone placement (and how discreet wireless mics work).
2. **"How I Wait"**: Why the videographer should not tell the couple to "smile" or "re-do the kiss", but quietly anticipate genuine micro-moments.
3. **Timeline Planning**: Tips on golden hour lighting without cutting into guest cocktail time.
4. **Questions to Ask Your Videographer**: Educating couples on contract questions (audio backups, raw footage, delivery milestones).
