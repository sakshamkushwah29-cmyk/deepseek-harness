# Platform Rulebook: Instagram Reels, Carousels, & Stories

## Strategic Role of Instagram
Instagram is the creator's home base. It is a visual credibility portfolio where high-net-worth clients (engaged couples booking luxury weddings and founders evaluating commercial production) vet taste, poise, and authenticity.

---

## 1. Reels (Talking Head & Cinematic B-Roll)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (1080 x 1920).
- **Target Duration**: 45 to 75 seconds. Long enough to deliver genuine depth, short enough to sustain watch-through rate.
- **Visual Pacing ("One Long Hold")**:
  - Avoid frantic zoom-ins, fast jump cuts, or sound effects every 2 seconds.
  - Hold the frame for 4-8 seconds per shot. Let the viewer observe your posture, facial expression, and surroundings.
  - Insert the signature observational cut: **[How I wait]** — a quiet 2-second hold of you standing with the camera, observing a bride, or waiting for natural light to hit an office window.
- **Audio Profile**:
  - Primary: Creator's natural speaking voice with warm room presence (lavalier or shotgun mic, minimal hyper-compression).
  - Background: Subtle low-volume ambient score or silence-then-hit. Never let loud trending music drown out the voice.

---

## 2. Carousels (Deep-Dive Educational Value)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 4:5 (1080 x 1350) for maximum vertical feed real-estate.
- **Structure**: 5 to 7 slides.
  - Slide 1: Bold, clean hook slide with high contrast title.
  - Slides 2-4: One core actionable insight per slide (e.g., "The 3 audio mistakes that ruin wedding speeches", "Why business founders look stiff on camera").
  - Slide 5: The "Behind the Lens" breakdown or counter-intuitive conclusion.
  - Final Slide: Low-friction save/share prompt focused on value.

---

## 3. POV Overlays (Value in Caption)
- **Visual**: 5 to 9 second looping b-roll of you operating a camera, setting up a tripod, color grading on a monitor, or patiently waiting at a shoot.
- **Overlay Text**: 1-2 sentence thought-provoking premise (e.g., *"Most videographers shoot what happens. We shoot what almost went unnoticed."*).
- **Caption**: The real meat. 150-300 words of thorough, honest breakdown answering a burning question or sharing a hard-won lesson.
- **Audio**: Original audio or subtle trending ambient sound.

---

## Algorithm Signals & Metrics
- **Save Rate**: The #1 metric for Middle-Layer trust. Carousels and educational breakdowns drive saves.
- **DM Shares**: Friends sending to their fiancé or co-founder ("We need someone like this").
- **Watch Time**: Driven by the calm, hypnotic "One Long Hold" style that stands out against noisy, frenetic feeds.
